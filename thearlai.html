Thearlaich! Nan Tigeadh Tu

Charlie, if you would only come!

Tune

Sequenced by Ch.Souchon


No Text known

Scottish Reel.

First published as two-part tune in the "Bremner Collection" (c.1770).
The last two parts are attributed to the Cape Breton fiddler
