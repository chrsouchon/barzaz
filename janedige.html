Janedig Flamm (en)

Joan the Arsonist

Tune
(Mi majeur)



1. -What is moving over the hill?
A flock of black sheep, I think;

2. -T'is not a flock of black sheep;
Soldiers, I don't gainsay,

3. French soldiers coming
To besiege Hennebont.-

4. As the duchess proceeded,
The city bells set swinging.

5. She rode on her white palfrey
With her son on her knee...


8. As the procession finished,
The French were heard crying:

9. -Caught will be now in their den
The hind and the fawn, all alive!

10. We have brought golden bonds
To chain them together.-

11. Duchess Joan answered
From the top of the tower:

12. It's not the hind that will be caught,
But the bad wolf , to be sure-...


15 She doned an iron cuirass,
And put on a black helmet;

16 And seized a sharp steel sword
And chose three hundred men,

17. With a glowing brand in her hand
She walked out of the city precinct.

18. The French were at that time
Merrily revelling and singing...

25. Many of them were slumped
Over the table, blind drunk,

26 Suddenly a cry of alarm was heard:
"Fire! Boys,fire! fire!

27. Fire! fire ! let's flee, boys!
Fire lit by Joan the Arsonist!..."

29. Joan had set fire
On the four corners of the camp;

30. And the wind had spread it around
And the dark night was brightly lit;

31. And the tents were all aflame,
And the French roasted,

32. And three thousand burnt to ashes,
Only hundred could escape...


36. Arson Joan smiled next morning:
"What a fine weed burning, my God!

37. My God! what a fine weed burning!
For one grain sown we'll reap ten!

38. They told the truth, in former times:
Nothing like a heap of French bones,

39. A heap of French bones, well ground,
To make our wheat come up!"

(Transl. Ch.Souchon (c)2003)



In 1341, whilst Duke John of Monfort, supported by England and acknowledged by the States of Brittany as their rightful ruler, was prisoner in Paris, his wife, Joan of Flanders, was besieged in Hennebont by Charles of Blois, leader of the French party. She had the amazing audacity, to set fire, herself, on the camp of the enemies, which brought her the nickname "Janedig Flamm (Joan the Arsonist)".
