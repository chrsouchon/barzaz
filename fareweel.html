Fareweel to a' our Scottish fame


Text by Robert Burns


Melody unknown

1. Fareweel to a' our Scottish fame,
Fareweel our ancient glory;
Fareweel to e'en our Scottish name
Sae famed in sang and story...
Now Sark runs tae th' Solway sands,
And Tweed runs t'th'ocean..
Tae mark whaur England's Province stands:
Sic a parcel of rogues in a nation!

2. What force or guile could not subdue
Through many warlike ages
Is wrought now by a coward few
For hireling traitor's wages.
The English steel we could disdain,
Secure in valor's station.
But English gold has been our bane:
Sic a parcel of rogues in a nation! 		3. Oh, would or had I seen the day
That treason thus could sell us!
My auld grey head had lain in clay
W'Bruce and loyal Wallace!
But, pith and power, till my last hour,
I'll make this declaration:
We were bought and sold for English gold!
Sic a parcel of rogues in a nation!
