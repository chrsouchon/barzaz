The Marquess of Guérand

Barzhaz Breizh

Ton
(Même mélodie que Baron Jauioz )

I
Bliss and joy to all house-dwellers;
Where is Annaïk, is she there?

-In her bed she sleeps and she rests
Mind you don't wake her; be quiet!

She lies all asleep in her bed;
Take care not to awaken her.-

The clerk of Garlan didn't care,
And immediately went upstairs.

Quick and briskly upstairs he went,
And sat down on the girl's bed-bench.

-Get up quickly, Annaïk Kalvez,
A new threshing floor waits for us!

-I won't go to this festival
For there will be there a rascal;

A nobleman, most mischievous,
Who after me always chases.

-If there were there a hundred lords,
To harm you no one could afford!

Even if hundred lords are there,
We'll go to the new threshing floor!

To the new threshing floor we'll go,
And we'll dance, the same as they do!-

Now she put on her woolen dress,
And followed her friend to the feast.

II
Marquess of Guérand was asking
The innkeeper, in the morning:

- Innkeeper, tell me, innkeeper,
Did you see the clerk anywhere ?

- Lord Marquess, will you excuse me,
For I do not know whom you mean.

-Excuse you! I am sure I won't!
I do mean the clerk of Garlan!

- He's gone to spend the day down there
With him a girl pretty and fair.

They've gone to the new threshing floor;
A pretty couple, to be sure !

On his hat a peacock feather
And a chain round his neck he wears;

He wears round his neck a long chain
A long chain that hangs down his breast.

A small velvet bodice she wears,
Embroidered over with silver,

That's in fact a wedding bodice;
They are engaged, to my belief.-

III
Now the marquess, in wild anger,
Has jumped up on his red charger;

Has jumped up on his red charger,
And off to the new threshing floor.

- Clerk, quickly, take off your doublet,
We will wrestle for the forfeit !

C lerc, quickly your doublet take off,
We'll try to trip each other up.

- With your leave, Marquess, I will not,
You call yourself a lord, I don't;
To Lady Guérand you are son,
As to me, I'm but a peasant.

- Although you are but a peasant,
You may choose any girl you want.

- My lord Marquess, please, consider:
I was not the one who chose her.

Marquess Guérand, I must gainsay,
It's God who gave her to me.-

Annaik Kalvez was trembling,
When she heard all that quarrelling :

- Be quiet, my friend and let us go;
He'll cause us but pain and sorrow. -

Before you leave, clerk, still a word:
Do you know how to hold a sword?

- Never did I hold a sword, but
I can fight with a stick, you don't!

- What about a stick fight with me?
You're said a real fighter to be!

- Lord nobleman, my poor cudgel
To your sharp sword is not equal!

I'll do, Lord, nothing of the like,
You would spoil your sword at each strike .

- If my sword is spoiled or dirty,
A good cleanser your blood will be!-

Annaik saw her friend's blood flow,
And flew in anger at his throat,

She grasped the marquess by the hair,
Dragged him on the floor all over.

- Be off, get away, vile traitor;
You're my darling clerk's murderer! -

IV
Annaik Kalvez went back home,
With her eyes full of tears she's come.

- My dear mother, if you love me,
You will prepare my bed quickly;

You'll prepare a very soft bed,
For my poor heart is afflicted.

- You've been dancing too much, daughter;
That's with your heart all the matter.

- I did not dance too much, doubtless:
He was killed by the cruel marquess!

The traitorous Marquess Guérand
Has killed my clerk with his own hand!

You shall say to the gravedigger,
When he comes to take him over:

« The grave you'll dig, please, don't fill in,
Soon shall my daughter follow him. »

We did not sleep in the same bed,
But we shall sleep in the same grave;

We were not married in this world,
But shall be married before God.-







Transl. Ch. Souchon(c)2003




Résumé
Towards 1670 young Marquess Guérand, Lord of Locmaria, kills on a new threshing floor festival , a clerk whose fiancée, Annaik Calvez, he covets.
